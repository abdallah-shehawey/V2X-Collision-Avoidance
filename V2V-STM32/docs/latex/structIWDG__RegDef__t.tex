\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
IWDG register block — APB1 @ 0x40003000, clocked by the LSI (\texorpdfstring{$\sim$}{\string~}32 k\+Hz). 

The IWDG is deliberately hard to disarm:\+ its registers are write-\/protected, and a magic key must be written to \doxylink{structIWDG__RegDef__t_a5570985723cddb8ac076b566bb0f9585}{IWDG\+\_\+\+Reg\+Def\+\_\+t::\+\+KR} to unlock them. Once the watchdog has been started it cannot be stopped at all — only reset before it expires. That is the whole point:\+ a runaway program must not be able to switch off its own safety net.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Its clock is the LSI, an RC oscillator accurate to roughly ±50\%, so the real timeout can be substantially shorter or longer than the nominal one. Timeouts here are chosen with generous margin rather than to the millisecond. 
\end{DoxyNote}
