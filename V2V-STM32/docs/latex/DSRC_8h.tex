\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The V2V transport:\+ the neighbor table and the over-\/the-\/air frame format shared with the ESP32 radio bridge. 

\begin{DoxyAuthor}{Author}
Abdallah Shehawey 
\end{DoxyAuthor}
DSRC is the layer every ADAS module sits on. It answers one question — {\itshape which other vehicles are near me right now, and what are they doing} — and keeps the answer in a table of at most \doxylink{group__app__dsrc_ga2b1d39159e4114f433745f15a201c93c}{MAX\+\_\+\+NEIGHBORS} entries, one per vehicle heard from recently.\hypertarget{DSRC_8h_dsrc_flow}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{The path a message takes}{The path a message takes}}\label{DSRC_8h_dsrc_flow}
The STM32 has no radio of its own. An ESP32 bridges the air interface:\+

\begin{DoxyVerb}*   other car --ESP-NOW--> our ESP32 --UART1--> STM32   (receive)
*   STM32 --UART1--> our ESP32 --ESP-NOW--> other cars  (transmit)
* \end{DoxyVerb}


On the way in, USART1\textquotesingle{}s ISR only enqueues raw bytes; \doxylink{group__app__dsrc_ga8f2c7037352677729c777df880fefd1b}{DSRC\+\_\+\+Rx\+Callback} is then driven from {\ttfamily \doxylink{main_8c_a8e83024643794f31ab8139cbdb1de5dd}{v\+Task\+\_\+\+ESP\+\_\+\+Comm}} in task context, one byte at a time, and reassembles frames. On the way out, \doxylink{group__app__dsrc_gaded740c50de5ea4cf786939e10ace2d9}{DSRC\+\_\+\+Send\+Neighbor} frames this vehicle\textquotesingle{}s own \doxylink{structNeighbor}{Neighbor} struct and hands it to the ESP32 every 100 ms.\hypertarget{DSRC_8h_dsrc_frame}{}\doxysubsection{\texorpdfstring{Frame layout}{Frame layout}}\label{DSRC_8h_dsrc_frame}
\begin{DoxyVerb}*   +------------+---------------------------+----------+----------+
*   | START_BYTE |   Neighbor (21 bytes)     | checksum | END_BYTE |
*   |    0xAA    |        packed             |  1 byte  |   0x55   |
*   +------------+---------------------------+----------+----------+
* \end{DoxyVerb}


A frame is only accepted if the start byte, the checksum and the end byte all agree, so a truncated or corrupted frame is dropped rather than acted on.

\begin{DoxyWarning}{Warning}
The \doxylink{structNeighbor}{Neighbor} layout is a {\bfseries{wire format}}, mirrored byte for byte in the ESP32 sketches ({\ttfamily esp32/\+master}, {\ttfamily esp32/\+sniffer}). Reordering, resizing or inserting a field on one side and not the other silently misaligns every field after it — the link keeps working and the data becomes nonsense. Change both sides together, always. 
\end{DoxyWarning}
