\doxysubsubsubsection*{Direction classification thresholds}
How far two vehicles\textquotesingle{} headings may differ and still count as travelling the same way, opposite ways, or across each other \+[degrees]\+.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
These were widened from 30° to 40° deliberately. Each car reports the heading {\itshape its own} magnetometer measured, and two magnetometers rarely agree to better than a few tens of degrees. A tight threshold therefore kept classifying genuinely same-\/direction pairs as \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_gga2e9ec086ba61b35cfc2439306cdfdd70a595694ddc9fc69be39448c7aebc29492}{DIR\+\_\+\+UNKNOWN}, which silently switched every cooperative module off. 
\end{DoxyNote}

\doxysubsubsubsection*{Shared safe-\/distance model}
The one place the firmware decides how much room is "{}enough"{}.

Both distance-\/based modules — local FCW and EEBL — use it, so a change here moves both, and the two can never drift out of agreement.

The gap scales with speed, because stopping distance does:\+

\begin{DoxyVerb}*   safe_cm     = max(Host_Speed[m/s] * SAFE_DIST_PER_MS, MIN_SAFE_DISTANCE)
*   critical_cm = safe_cm * CRITICAL_RATIO
* \end{DoxyVerb}


\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
The constants are sized for the {\bfseries{prototype}}, not a real car:\+ a small vehicle whose top speed is around 0.\+5 m/\+s, operating in corridors where obstacles sit tens of centimetres away. At that scale the speed term is small and \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_gae17b0ade1e41ab06c55995e9afee25ff}{MIN\+\_\+\+SAFE\+\_\+\+DISTANCE} is what actually governs — it is the dominant floor, not a rarely-\/hit corner case. 
\end{DoxyNote}

void Safety\+Engine\+\_\+void\+Update (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}\item[{void}]{}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})}



Run one full ADAS cycle:\+ every module, over every neighbor, in one pass. 

This is the ADAS brain, and it is the only place a hazard decision is made. Each 50 ms cycle it:\+


\begin{DoxyEnumerate}
\item latches \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_ga16174df02da93c0fc96432a4bc1de57b}{Host\+\_\+\+Speed} and \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_gafb1ff5cedbd39f1a8b81a25a7dcdb171}{Host\+\_\+\+Heading}, and computes this cycle\textquotesingle{}s \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_ga2d869610528b06238c15ab8fb160304f}{Safety\+Engine\+\_\+\+Safe\+Dist} and \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_ga414ebc77c31778b0d2f7371abdbd2d19}{Safety\+Engine\+\_\+\+Critical\+Dist};
\item walks the DSRC neighbor table {\bfseries{once}}, offering each neighbor to every module that its \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_ga2e9ec086ba61b35cfc2439306cdfdd70}{Direction\+\_\+t} makes relevant;
\item aggregates the modules\textquotesingle{} \doxylink{group__app__safetyengine_gaa870174b113e13762b47e719bf3efa88}{Risk\+Level\+\_\+t} results into \doxylink{group__system_ga9e78367aa5b758af972838362c2b0eab}{G\+\_\+u16\+System\+Flags}.
\end{DoxyEnumerate}

The single pass matters:\+ the modules share the neighbor table and the latched host state, so walking it once means they all reason about one coherent snapshot of the world rather than five slightly different ones.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Makes no actuator decision whatsoever. It publishes a status word; {\ttfamily \doxylink{main_8c_a96452f43e9871212929e6e6a969bec13}{v\+Task\+\_\+\+Feedback}} is what turns that into light and sound. 
\end{DoxyNote}
\begin{DoxyWarning}{Warning}
The caller must hold {\bfseries{both}} mutexes, taken in the order Neighbor\+Table → Data. 
\end{DoxyWarning}
