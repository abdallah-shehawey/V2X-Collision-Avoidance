Evaluate one same-\/direction neighbor against the rear gap. 

Process one SAME-\/direction DSRC neighbor for EEBL. The Safety\+Engine only dispatches same-\/direction neighbors here.

Risk depends on the host\textquotesingle{}s own speed (the cycle safe/\+critical gaps), not on relative speed:\+ a sudden brake with a vehicle close behind raises a warning. Keeps the worst severity across the cycle.
