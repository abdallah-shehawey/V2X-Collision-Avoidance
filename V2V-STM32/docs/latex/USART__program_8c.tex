\doxymbox{\hyperlink{group__lib_ga74f58890b599c641e4382ff49c4a3e81}{Error\+State\+\_\+t}} USART\+\_\+\+Init\+IT (\begin{DoxyParamCaption}\item[{\doxymbox{\hyperlink{structUSART__Handle__t}{USART\+\_\+\+Handle\+\_\+t}} \texorpdfstring{$\ast$}{*}}]{Channel\+Handle}{}\end{DoxyParamCaption})}



Bring a USART up in {\bfseries{interrupt-\/driven}} mode. 

Applies the same line settings as \doxylink{USART__intreface_8h_a98f967289c40eacb31068b3becfff84f}{USART\+\_\+\+Init}, and additionally enables the interrupt sources named in the handle and registers its callback. This is what USART1 uses, since the ESP32 transmits unprompted.


\begin{DoxyParams}[1]{Parameters}
\doxymbox{\texttt{in}}  & {\em Channel\+Handle} & The port\textquotesingle{}s settings, interrupt enables and ISR callback. \\
\hline
\end{DoxyParams}
\begin{DoxyReturn}{Returns}
OK if the port was configured, NULL\+\_\+\+POINTER if {\ttfamily Channel\+Handle} was NULL.
\end{DoxyReturn}
\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
Enabling the interrupt here is only half the job — the IRQ must also be enabled in the NVIC (\doxylink{NVIC__interface_8h_a85d98abbd23523e4abf16cc4b4501431}{NVIC\+\_\+v\+Enable\+IRQ}), and given a priority number of 6 or higher if its callback touches the Free\+RTOS API. 
\end{DoxyNote}
