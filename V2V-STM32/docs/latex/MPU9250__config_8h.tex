\begin{Indent}\textbf{ Magnetometer hard-\/iron calibration}\par
{\em "{}\+Hard iron"{} is the constant magnetic offset the car itself adds — the motors, the battery, the steel chassis.

It shifts the whole field reading off centre, so an uncalibrated compass reads a heading that is simply wrong, and wrong by a different amount in every direction.

The fix is to turn the car through a full circle at boot and watch the field trace out a ring; the centre of that ring {\itshape is} the offset. That is what these three constants govern. }\begin{DoxyCompactItemize}
