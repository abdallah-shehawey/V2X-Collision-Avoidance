{\bfseries{Echo level (5 V → 3.\+3 V):\+}} the echo pins (PA15, PB3, PB4, PB5, PC8, PC9) are all {\bfseries{5 V-\/tolerant}} on the F446 and do work directly on the Nucleo — but on the PCB, place an {\bfseries{optional divider footprint}} anyway:\+ R1 = 1 kΩ (echo → pin) and R2 = 2 kΩ (pin → GND).
