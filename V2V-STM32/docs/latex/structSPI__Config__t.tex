\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Everything needed to bring one SPI peripheral up. 

The fields map one-\/to-\/one onto bits of the SPI {\ttfamily CR1}/\+{\ttfamily CR2} registers, so this struct is essentially a typed, readable form of those registers.

\begin{DoxyNote}{Note}
{\ttfamily \doxylink{structSPI__Config__t_a3eb827d937f1d50611d731b85f7a1119}{CPOL}} and {\ttfamily \doxylink{structSPI__Config__t_a7ecef74a0f8f84d8050f8c655c600361}{CPHA}} together choose the SPI {\itshape mode} (0..3), and they must match what the slave expects or every byte comes back garbage. The MPU9250 on this board wants mode 0 — \doxylink{SPI__interface_8h_a34beb9edf30f0d6974c2819a941cc423ad12b15db33ea03dc6d722c86bfdaaca2}{SPI\+\_\+\+CPOL\+\_\+\+LOW} with \doxylink{SPI__interface_8h_a78da88ed6e4be342886ae4e703454ff9abf6e4c4ae342ea61214abbaa51ca3912}{SPI\+\_\+\+CPHA\+\_\+1\+EDGE}. 
\end{DoxyNote}
