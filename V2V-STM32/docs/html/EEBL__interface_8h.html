Process one SAME-direction DSRC neighbor for EEBL. The SafetyEngine only dispatches same-direction neighbors here.

Risk depends on the host's own speed (the cycle safe/critical gaps), not on relative speed: a sudden brake with a vehicle close behind raises a warning. Keeps the worst severity across the cycle.
