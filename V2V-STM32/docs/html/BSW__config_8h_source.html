6 * SENDER: uses its FRONT-side ultrasonics (front-left / front-right) to
7 * detect a car alongside-ahead of it, and broadcasts bsw_flag as a
8 * bitmask (bit0=LEFT, bit1=RIGHT) of the side(s) it saw.
9 * RECEIVER: for each side bit set, checks the OPPOSITE REAR sensor (sender
10 * LEFT -> my rear-right, sender RIGHT -> my rear-left). If something
11 * is there, this car sits in the sender's blind spot -> raise BSW on
12 * that side. Both bits are handled independently.
